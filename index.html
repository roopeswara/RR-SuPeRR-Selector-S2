Royals Pressure Reliability Index (RPRI) Pipeline

Rather than relying on static over filters (such as blindly labeling Overs 16-20 as 'pressure'), our ML pipeline reconstructs the exact pre-delivery match state for all 200,999 deliveries in the RR proprietary dataset. We compute a continuous dynamic leverage score $PI_b \in [0, 1]$ applying symmetrically to both batter and bowler.

1 Dynamic Leverage Math

Evaluates required run rate acceleration, rolling 18-ball wicket clusters ($W_{18b}$), scoreboard stagnation, and endgame closeness ($\lambda_{endgame}$).

2 Empirical Bayes Shrinkage

Prevents associate-nation or small-sample stat padding by regularizing raw pressure metrics toward qualified population means with credibility weighting $\omega = n / (n + k)$.

3 Workload & Health Audit

Audits 2025-2026 match load (min 15 matches across 2 seasons) and medical conditions (Achilles, groin, back load) for IPL 2027 auction viability.

Innings 1 & 2 Leverage Models

Innings 1 (Collapse Recovery & Stage Escalation):
PI1,b = 0.45·min(1, 0.35·W18b + 0.65·(Wcum/8)) + 0.35·Sphase + 0.20·max(0, min(1, (9.0-CRR)/4))
Innings 2 & Super Over (Chase Leverage):
PI2,b = 0.45·min(1, RRR/13.5) + 0.30·min(1, 0.35·W18b + 0.65·(Wcum/8)1.3) + 0.25·exp(-|RRR-CRR|/3.5)·λendgame

Match Multipliers: IPL = 1.08x quality multiplier; Playoffs/Knockouts = 1.08x stakes multiplier.

RPRI Composite Z-Scores Formula

RPRIBat = 0.30(zEB_SR) + 0.20(zEB_Avg) + 0.15(zEB_Bnd) - 0.15(zEB_Dot) + 0.10(zStepUp) + 0.10(zCons)

RPRIBowl = -0.30(zEB_Eco) - 0.25(zEB_SR) + 0.15(zEB_Dot) - 0.15(zEB_Bnd) + 0.10(zSpell) + 0.05(zEcoSave)

Metrics are normalized using standardized Z-scores and scaled to a 0–100 index. Consistency measures match-to-match reliability (batters SR ≥ 140; bowlers spell economy ≤ 8.50).
